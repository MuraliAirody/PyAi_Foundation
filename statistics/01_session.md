# Statistics

Statistics is the branch of mathematics concerned with collecting, organizing, analyzing, interpreting, and presenting data to make decisions or draw conclusions under uncertainty.

In simpler terms:
Statistics helps you understand data and make sense of variability.

- Why statistics is important

Statistics is used to:

- Identify patterns and trends in data

- Make predictions and forecasts

- Support decision-making based on evidence

- Quantify uncertainty and risk

You see it everywhere: business reports, medical studies, stock markets, machine learning models, surveys, and experiments.

## Main branches of statistics
### 1. Descriptive Statistics

Focuses on summarizing and describing data.

Examples:

Mean (average), median, mode

Range, variance, standard deviation

Tables, charts, histograms

Example:
“The average salary of employees is ₹50,000 with a standard deviation of ₹8,000.”

### 2. Inferential Statistics

Uses sample data to make conclusions about a population.

Examples:

Probability

Hypothesis testing

Confidence intervals

Regression analysis

Example:
“Based on a sample of 1,000 voters, we predict the election result with 95% confidence.”
